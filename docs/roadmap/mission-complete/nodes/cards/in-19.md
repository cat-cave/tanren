# in-19 — A3 live trigger/observe and effect probe

## Goal

Close the deliberately fail-closed in-17 gap for a product integration whose
`criticality` is `release_required`: after a verified deployment, Tanren must
drive the real declared HTTP cause, independently inspect the provider-side
effect at the exact production binding coordinate, append redacted immutable
observation evidence, and block delivery when the effect is missing, ambiguous,
duplicated, or bound to a different resource/generation.

The first concrete provider is direct product Slack. It uses the product-plane
`conversations.history` capability against the immutable production binding's
exact `external_resource_id`; it never treats the product HTTP response as an
effect receipt.

## Production call graph

`bootRunWorker → buildDemoOnDeployWatcher → DemoOnDeployWatcher →
buildProofBackedWebDemo → AcceptanceOrchestrator → CausalCorrelationStage →
HttpAcceptanceCauseDriver → deployed product HTTP endpoint`, with the matching
`LiveSlackEffectProbe` provider read returning through the causal reader,
appending immutable evidence/events, and feeding the recorded behavior verdict.
The in-17 `DeliveryDagDriver` is the real producer: its `stimulate/observe`
stages invoke the demo watcher under the existing intent-marker and advisory
lock fence. The `record_evidence` stage may complete a release-required delivery
only after that exact A3 verdict is present.

## Authority and fail-closed rules

- The trigger is a real declared HTTP probe. It receives fresh correlation and
  idempotency digests; a cause without a matching probe or a provider watermark
  is unavailable, never fabricated.
- The Slack probe authorizes `conversations.history` under a product-plane grant,
  reads the exact binding generation's channel only, rejects blank/wrong-type
  provider fields, and stores hashes/IDs rather than text or credentials.
- Provider observations count only when their correlation digest is present,
  their provider cursor is strictly after the pre-trigger watermark, and their
  channel/resource and binding generation are the exact expected coordinate.
- A successful complete provider history read that finds no matching digest
  appends durable `missing` evidence. That is the only observed-zero state;
  provider I/O errors, malformed pages, incomplete pagination, unresolved/multiple
  requirement bindings, and an absent correlation produce no assertion
  observation and therefore a blocking verdict.
- The existing in-17 intent marker remains the final durable write before the
  non-idempotent live trigger. Intent-without-terminal degrades and never fires
  again.

## Negative controls

1. A positive trigger with no provider-side message records `missing` evidence
   and fails its exactly-one/cardinality assertion; it never passes on an empty
   set.
2. A negative trigger receives a complete provider history read with zero exact
   correlations and may satisfy `has_no_effect`; an unreadable or paginated /
   malformed provider result produces an unexecuted blocking verdict instead.
3. A Slack message in another channel, at/before the pre-trigger cursor, or with
   another correlation digest is excluded and cannot satisfy the assertion.
4. A missing/ambiguous production binding set, stale generation, or missing
   provider `conversations.history` authority blocks before a provider request.
5. A repeated correlation is persisted as duplicate evidence and cannot satisfy
   an exactly-once/unique assertion.
